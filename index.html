Frequently Asked Questions

Why does this converter produce sharper images than standard tools?

Most online converters use fixed server-side renderers that rasterize vector files at low default pixel densities. Our browser-based canvas multiplies the SVG viewBox dimensions prior to rendering, preserving crisp edges at up to 8x resolution.

What if my SVG does not have width or height attributes?

Our parser reads the vector's viewBox attribute to automatically determine the natural aspect ratio and dimensions, preventing clipped or distorted renders.

Can I copy the PNG straight to my clipboard without downloading?

Yes. Clicking Copy Image utilizes the modern asynchronous Clipboard API to write native PNG pixel data to your clipboard for instant pasting.

Does this converter work completely offline?

Yes. All vector calculations and canvas raster operations run locally without requiring an internet connection.
